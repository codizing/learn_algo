About the Platform

About Learn Algo with MR

Built by a student, for students learning Algorithmique — because scattered PDFs in random group chats deserve better organization.

The Mission

One Organized Hub for CS Students

Learn Algo with MR exists to give Computer Science students from L1 to L3 one organized place for everything related to Algorithmique — courses, TDs, TPs, exams, quizzes, and more.

No more digging through scattered Facebook groups, Telegram channels, or old conversations every time exam season comes around.

Everything you need, organized in one place.
The Story

Born from Real Student Experience

Learn Algo with MR was created by a Computer Science student who experienced firsthand how difficult it can be to find, organize, and keep track of Algorithmique resources.

Between different PDFs, TDs shared across multiple groups, recorded lessons, and files saved somewhere you can never seem to find again, studying Algorithmique can quickly become more complicated than it needs to be.

So I decided to build the platform I wished I had as a student — a simple, organized space where you can learn, practice, and keep track of your progress.

And now, I'm sharing it with other students facing the same struggle.
